# Competing hypotheses

Listed alphabetically; order implies no ranking. Each must be expressed as a testable scenario
(final-behaviour class in the simulator) and none receives a prior until priors are documented.

- **H-HOW-SINK**: Aircraft ran out of fuel and ditched near Howland; sank.
- **H-LOP-337**: Continued/searched along the 337° direction of the LOP.
- **H-LOP-157**: Continued/searched along the 157° direction of the LOP.
- **H-NIKU**: Landed on or near Nikumaroro (Gardner Island) — physically plausible only if reachable
  given fuel/navigation; to be tested, not assumed.
- **H-OTHER**: Other justified patterns (e.g. box/spiral search, Mili/Marshalls, etc.).

Required for each: reachability under fuel constraints, consistency with radio evidence,
what the 1937 search would have seen, and modern search coverage that bears on it.
